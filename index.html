Enterprise AI Acceptable Use & Governance Policy

Executive Policy Standard (v1.0)

1. Scope: Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors evaluating or deploying Generative AI, Machine Learning, or Autonomous Agent workflows.

2. Shadow AI Prohibition: Confidential company data, PII, financial records, and proprietary code must never be entered into unapproved public AI interfaces.

3. Tiered Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Mandate:
• Tier 1 (Internal Productivity): Enterprise Zero-Data-Retention tools only; standard employee verification.
• Tier 2 (External / Operational): Requires AI Council sign-off and mandatory human review prior to customer release.
• Tier 3 (Autonomous Agents / Regulated): Requires formal Risk Assessment, red-teaming, sandboxed permissions, and deterministic kill switches.
